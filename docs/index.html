How the agent works

Generate a candidate from a common word. A critic checks it: every word can be spelled, no offensive word can be formed from the letters, no obscure required words, 4 to 12 words, one word uses every letter, no repeats. The agent repairs what it can (obscure words become optional bonus words) and rejects the rest. Accepted levels are placed on a planned difficulty curve with an easier level every fifth.

The difficulty score was checked against a separate simulated player. My first score failed that check, so I replaced it: details in the README.
